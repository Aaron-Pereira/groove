▶ How this works

Random album by year

  • Pick a year and groove rolls a random album from Billboard's Year-End Top 200 Albums chart for that year (MusicBrainz is used for years Billboard doesn't cover).
  • Albums already in your library or download queue are excluded automatically.
  • Nothing is downloaded until you hit Accept & download — roll again as many times as you like.
  • Accepted albums are downloaded from YouTube Music as a full album and filed under Artist/Album (Year).
